# A greeting for new contributors

G1: People who open the repository for the first time are welcomed and told they are free to try anything here, since the repository is a playground for experimenting where nothing is meant to last.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: In `README.md`, after the description line and a blank line, a reader finds the line "Welcome, new contributors! Feel free to try anything here, and see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.", and its link opens `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- AC2: `CONTRIBUTING.md` at the top of the repository tells a newcomer, in a few lines under a heading, how to start: make a branch, try anything, and open a pull request, with no need to be careful since nothing here is meant to last.
