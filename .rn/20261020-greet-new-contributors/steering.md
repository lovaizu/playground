# A greeting for new contributors

G1: People who come to the repository for the first time are welcomed when they open the README, so that they know it is a place where they can feel free to try things.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: The README, right after its description of the repository and with a blank line between, reads "Welcome, new contributors — feel free to try anything here. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.", its link opening CONTRIBUTING.md.
- AC2: CONTRIBUTING.md, at the top of the repository, says under the heading "Contributing" that anyone may make a branch and try anything freely, that changes are sent as pull requests, and that nothing here is meant to last.
