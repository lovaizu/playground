# What the checklist for CONTRIBUTING says

## User and purpose

A CONTRIBUTING file is read by a new contributor, coming from the README's link, who from it alone makes their first change and sends it as a pull request, so they can act as soon as they finish reading.

## Items

The checklist asks four things, each what the reader gets, with usability.md's points the reader needs as signs under them: on opening, they can tell the file is for them; with what they have, they make their first change by its steps; they send it as a pull request and know what happens next; and each statement holds when they try it. It asks no rules of conduct or style, which the repository does not yet have.
