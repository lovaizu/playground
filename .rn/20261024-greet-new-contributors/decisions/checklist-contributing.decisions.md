# What the checklist for CONTRIBUTING says

## User and purpose

A CONTRIBUTING file is read by a new contributor, coming from the README's link, who from it alone makes their first change and sends it as a pull request, so they can act as soon as they finish reading.

## Items

The checklist asks four things, each what the reader gets, with usability.md's points the reader needs as signs under them: on opening, they can tell the file is for them; with what they have, they make their first change by its steps; they send it as a pull request and know what happens next; and each statement holds when they try it.

## Shape

The checklist is in rn's template for checklists, so rn's scripts and turn read it as any other: front matter naming contributing, version 1, made from checklist 1 and usability 1; an opening with the user and purpose, then the idea; a Format section whose F-1 gives the CONTRIBUTING file's template, a title, its main point under it, then a section for making the first change and one for sending it; and a Benefit section holding the four items as B-1 to B-4, each with its scope, a yes-or-no question, the signs to look for and why.
