# Greet new contributors in the README

G1: A first-time contributor knows they are welcome here and may try things freely, so they are willing to join in.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: The README has one new line that tells a newcomer both that they are welcome and that they may try things freely, and its two existing lines stay as they are.

## Tasks

- [ ] T1: Add that line to the README. Serves AC1.

  Completion criterion: Reading `README.md` shows one new line saying both points, with its two existing lines unchanged.

  - [ ] 1. Read `README.md`, finding out what its two lines say and where a new line fits; confirm with the user the wording the line should take. The orchestrator, with the user.
  - [ ] 2. Design the line, `README.md` as a document work, and check it against the two points of the completion criterion. turn, first run.
  - [ ] 3. Trace the design on paper, or try it, against its checks. That run's first reader.
  - [ ] 4. Make the line in `README.md`, checked by reading the file. turn, second run.
